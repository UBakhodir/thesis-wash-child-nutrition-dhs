## 2. Verification-depth labels corrected

**The defect**: v1's Section 3 occurrence ledger used the phrase "Full-text (abstract) read, prior pass" and variants ("Full-text (abstract/results) read," "Full-text (title/abstract) read") in at least 18 of its 48 rows. These phrases are self-contradictory — "full-text" and "abstract" describe different, mutually exclusive depths of reading — and should never have been combined this way.

**The fix**: `citation_map.md` (the per-source claim-verification record) has been rewritten with a single, consistent "Evidence depth" column using three non-overlapping categories, defined there explicitly:
- **Full-text passage inspected** — the specific supporting passage was read, with a page/section/figure/table locator where available.
- **Abstract inspected** — only the abstract (or title+abstract) was read, not the full body.
- **Indirect version correspondence** — applies only to `Blom2022`: evidence connects two versions of a source without a direct read of the cited version's own text.

Of the 20 cited sources: **12 are full-text passage inspected** (Victora2010, Black2013, Cumming2019, LBDWaSH2020, Donohue2023, Rakotomanana2020, Addae2024, HeadeyPalloni2019, GerusoSpears2015, Burgert2013, PerezHeydrich2013, deOnis2006); **7 are abstract inspected only** (Victora2008, CummingCairncross2016, SkoufiasVinha2026, Gunther2010, Spears2013, Balk2004, JohnsonJacobBrown2013); **1 is indirect version correspondence** (Blom2022). No evidence was upgraded to claim a fuller read than actually occurred; several rows that v1 ambiguously phrased as "Full-text (abstract)" were resolved to **"Abstract inspected"** specifically where no full-body passage or locator was actually ever recorded (e.g., Victora2008, Gunther2010, Spears2013, Balk2004, JohnsonJacobBrown2013), and to **"Full-text passage inspected"** where a specific locator or direct quote was already on record (e.g., Victora2010's results-section quote "falters dramatically until 24 months"; Black2013's p.434 Panel 1 citation).

Because evidence depth is a property of *how deeply each source was read*, not of each individual citation occurrence, this single per-key table in `citation_map.md` is now the authoritative record for all 48 occurrences' evidence depth; v1's Section 3 occurrence table (48 rows, each individually labelled) is superseded on this specific column by `citation_map.md` and should not be read for evidence-depth language — its claim/location/key/verdict columns remain accurate and unaffected.

**Blom2022 specifically** (explicit instruction: do not treat secondary coverage as a primary read): `citation_map.md`'s Blom2022 row and `bibliography_verification_ledger.md`'s Blom2022 row have both been re-checked and now state explicitly that (a) the published *JEEM* article's own abstract/full text has not been read directly in any pass to date; (b) the working paper's abstract, which has been read directly, is what actually supports the manuscript's matching-method claim; (c) the version-correspondence argument (same authors/order/method/sample, and secondary press coverage reporting the published version's effect sizes matching the working paper's own abstract) is evidence that the two versions correspond, stated as exactly that — correspondence evidence — and not elevated to "the published article was read." This was already the substantive position in v1; the wording in both files has been tightened this pass to remove any phrase that could be misread as claiming otherwise.

## 3. Name uncertainty: resolved with evidence, not inferred orthography, or explicitly left open with the source spelling documented

**`PerezHeydrich2013`** — v1 left the accent question "unresolved" while *keeping* the accented spelling, reasoning in part from "standard Spanish orthography." That reasoning is exactly what this task's instructions warn against (inferring a preferred spelling from general orthographic convention rather than checking a record for this specific person). The byte-level PDF check already performed (recorded in v1, §2.2) is itself the right kind of evidence — it checks the actual displayed byline — and it shows the DHS Spatial Analysis Report 8 title page rendering "Heydrich" with a plain "e," not "é," in both occurrences. No independent authoritative author record (ORCID, institutional page) was located to check further. Given the primary document's own byline is the best evidence actually in hand, `bibliography.bib`'s `author` field was changed to **"Perez-Heydrich, Carolina"** (unaccented), matching that document's own spelling, with a note disclosing that the accented form is common in secondary citations of this author's other work and that this is a record of the source document's own spelling, not an assertion about her generally preferred spelling. This is a resolution to the available primary-source evidence, not a claim that the matter is settled beyond it.

**`Momberg2021`** — v1 left the full given names unchanged ("unverifiable, not discrepant") while every source actually reached (Crossref, the publisher record) gave only initials.
