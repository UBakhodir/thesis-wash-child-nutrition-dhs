# Contributors

This is an individual Master's thesis project.

## Author

**Bakhodir Izzatulloev** — MSc Economics — sole researcher and author of the
thesis and the repository that accompanies it.

## Scope of work

The full pipeline in this repository was designed and built by Bakhodir
Izzatulloev, covering:

- DHS data preparation and harmonization for Ethiopia, Ghana, Kenya, and
  Nigeria (`scripts/dhs_harmonization/00`–`06`)
- Anthropometric outcome construction (HAZ, WAZ, WHZ, and the stunting,
  wasting, underweight, and severe indicators derived from them)
- WASH variable construction, including the household-level water and
  sanitation classification and the Ghana bio-digester treatment
- Leave-one-out cluster-level WASH exposure construction (`08`)
- Administrative-region fixed-effect linkage (`09`) and birth-order
  extraction (`10`)
- Main regression analysis (`11`) and the six robustness and
  sensitivity-analysis families (`12`)
- Final table, figure, and appendix production (`13`)
- Literature review organization and the accompanying evidence
  documentation under `sources/`
- The methodological documentation and reproducibility/provenance records
  under `docs/` and `outputs/final_audit/`

No other individuals contributed code, analysis, or writing to this
repository.
