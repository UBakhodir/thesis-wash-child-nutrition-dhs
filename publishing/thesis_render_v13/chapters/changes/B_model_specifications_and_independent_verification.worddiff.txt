{+**The SE difference in Table<nbsp>B.2 is fully explained; it is not residual numerical noise.** The declared small-sample correction of `16_estimate_preliminary.py` is $(G/(G-1)) \times ((N-1)/(N-K))$, applied to the raw cluster-robust sandwich, with confidence intervals and *p*-values from a Student *t* distribution on $G-1$ degrees of freedom. {+$$+}
{+\begin{aligned}+}
{+\frac{N}{N-K}\times\left(\frac{G}{G-1}\times\frac{N-1}{N}\right) &= \frac{G}{G-1}\times\frac{N-1}{N-K}.+}
{+\end{aligned}+}
{+$$+}
