5. Design-documentation check - IMPORTANT FINDING, REQUIRING A
   SPECIFICATION DECISION, NOT RESOLVED HERE:
   docs/thesis_design.md, Section 7.3 "Control Variables", lists exactly
   eight household/child-level controls: child age, child sex, birth
   order, mother's age, mother's education, household wealth quintile,
   household size, and urban/rural residence. "Literacy" does not appear
   anywhere in this list, under that name or any variant (a full-text
   search of thesis_design.md, data_dictionary.md, and the master
   project's internal docs/methodology_notes/ planning-notes folder (empty
   at the time of this check; not included in the shareable package) for
   "literacy"/"v155"/"read"/"reading" returned no matches other than the
   one already-quoted "Low maternal education flag" heterogeneity-
   moderator definition, which is a different, binary, not-yet-implemented
   variable for a later heterogeneity stage, not the currently-implemented
   `literacy` control).

   This means the open question is not merely "should literacy be 5-
   category or binary" (the framing in the original audit) - it is more
   fundamentally: literacy (v155) as a control variable at all does not
   appear in the currently documented 8-variable control-variable design.
   It may be a deliberate, reasonable enrichment beyond the original
   design (mother's education and literacy are related but distinct
   constructs, and DHS commonly collects both), or it may be an
   unintended addition that should be reconciled with thesis_design.md
   before the specification is treated as final. This is a substantive
   specification question, not a code-correctness question - it was
   intentionally left undecided and unchanged at this stage, and is
   listed as an open item in Section K below.
