## Sources in the 35-row audit intentionally excluded from `bibliography.bib`

- **S26, S27** (WHO Child Growth Standards: growth-velocity and head/arm-circumference/skinfold monographs). The audit itself flags that these two rows' saved PDF files and ISBNs appear to be **swapped relative to their title/year metadata** (S26's title says "growth velocity, 2009" but its verified note describes reading the head/arm-circumference monograph with a 2007 copyright page; S27's title says "head/arm circumference, 2007" but its verified note describes reading the growth-velocity monograph with a 2009 copyright page). Neither monograph covers HAZ/WAZ/WHZ, the outcomes this thesis actually uses (the audit says so explicitly for both), so neither is cited, and this file–metadata mismatch is recorded here as an outstanding data-hygiene item in the source audit CSV rather than resolved by this manuscript pass. **Recommended author action**: re-verify and, if confirmed swapped, correct the `pdf_filename`/ISBN fields for S26 and S27 in `sources/bibliography/bibliography_source_audit.csv` directly (not done here, to avoid altering a restricted/authoritative audit file without a dedicated review pass).
- **S14, S17, S20, S22, S25, S35** — verified but not yet drawn on in the chapter text; left in the audit CSV as a reserve pool, not included in `bibliography.bib` to keep the working bibliography matched to what is actually cited.

## Style

Author–year (parenthetical) citation style is used throughout, following standard economics convention; no university-specific style requirement was found locally (see the prior readiness audit, `audit_reports/thesis_readiness_audit_v1_2026-10-06.md`, outside this repository). DOIs are included in `bibliography.bib` for every source that has one; the DHS Spatial Analysis Report series and most institutional reports do not assign DOIs, confirmed in the source audit, and are cited by title and series number instead.
